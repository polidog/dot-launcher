A home screen app with no icon grid, just text. The home screen shows a big pixel clock, your notifications, recently used apps and the song that's playing — nothing else.

■ Type to launch
・Tap or swipe up on the home screen to search. Type a few letters and press Enter
・Results are ranked by prefix > word start > substring. Full-width/half-width and hiragana/katakana are treated the same
・Words you used to open an app are kept as history, so you can search again with a single tap

■ Home screen
・A big pixel-art clock and the date
・Your notifications (tap to open, swipe sideways to dismiss)
・Recently used apps
・The song that's playing scrolls along the bottom, with previous / play-pause / next buttons
・Swipe left for a page of all apps grouped by category (Social, Work, Maps & Travel, Games, ...)

■ Look
・Light mode looks like a 4-shade handheld console LCD; dark mode is a black game-style window with a white frame
・Toggle light/dark from the top-left corner, or follow the system setting

■ Jev (optional, bring your own API key)
Enter an API key for TypeSafe's Jev in settings to unlock:
・Search by meaning (type "music" to find your music app, "map" to find Maps)
・Automatic categories for apps whose developer didn't declare one
Get your key from typesafe.ai. Without a key, every core launcher feature still works. App names and search words are sent to TypeSafe only when you enter a key.

■ Permissions
・Notification access: to show notifications and the playing song on the home screen
・Usage access: to show recently used apps
Both are optional. The developer never receives any of this information.

■ Language
The interface is currently in Japanese.
