# Privacy Policy

[日本語](privacy.md)

Last updated: 2026-10-20

DotLauncher ("the app") is an Android home screen app developed and distributed by polidog.

## Information the developer receives

None. The app sends nothing to the developer's servers and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Information used only on your device

The app reads the following on your device to display it, and never sends it off the device.

| Information | Purpose | Permission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| List of installed apps | Search and the category page | None |
| Notification contents | Showing notifications on the home screen | Notification access |
| Title and artist of the playing song | Showing and controlling the playing song | Notification access |
| App usage | Showing recently used apps | Usage access |
| Search history (up to 10 entries), settings | The next search and display | None |

History and settings stay in the app's storage on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Information sent to TypeSafe (Jev)

Only if you enter a TypeSafe API key in settings, the app sends the following to TypeSafe's API (api.typesafe.ai) for search by meaning and automatic categories:

- Names and package names of installed apps
- Words typed in the search box

Requests are made with your own API key and are handled under TypeSafe's terms of service and privacy policy. Nothing is sent if you don't enter a key, and sending stops when you remove it.

The API key is stored only on your device.
